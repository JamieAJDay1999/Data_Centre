S1.2 Prices, calendar and settlement
The source is LCCC's IMRP actuals dataset [3], whose reference product is the Great Britain
day-ahead hourly price. Source elds identify local date, chronological hourly settlement period
and IMRP amount in GBP/MWh. The 2025 series covers 8,760 physical hours. Hourly prices
are replicated to four consecutive quarter-hours without smoothing, giving 35,040 committed
intervals. The timeline uses UTC for physical ordering and Europe/London for local-date and
workload-slot labels. Spring and autumn clock changes produce 92 and 100 quarter-hours in
those local days; workload values for skipped/repeated local-clock periods are correspondingly
skipped/repeated. No physical UTC interval is missing or duplicated.
Negative prices remain signed in both optimisation and reported costs. There are 672
negative-price quarter-hours in the retained annual data. IMRP is a reference-price signal, not
a full retail tari. The rst three hourly prices on 1 January 2026 are GBP 46.49, 40.19 and
28.99/MWh; they supply the twelve nal look-ahead intervals. The exact expanded central price
timeline is included asdata/price_timeline.csv. Annual cost uses only committed intervals
in calendar 2025.
S2 Annual evaluation and state transfer
Each annual case follows this procedure:
1. Build the continuous UTC price timeline and assign Europe/London date and prole-slot
labels. Load the quarter-hour workload and tranche arrays.
2. Initialise UPS/TES energy and the four thermal states once, at the beginning of the year;
the initial unnished-work ledger is empty.
3. For local dayd, select its 92, 96 or 100 core intervals and append twelve further intervals.
Load the carried state and unnished cohorts, and generate new arrivals throughout the
horizon.
4. Solve the reference or coordinated model, and record feasibility, objective bounds and
accounting checks. Both core and look-ahead contain the full facility demand.
5. Commit only core controls and processing. Accumulate signed-price settlement for those
intervals, save their ending physical state and compute unnished work from committed
processing only.
6. Pass the ending state and unnished cohorts to the next local day. Reoptimise all future
controls. Repeat through 31 December.

S3.3 Search, certication and dispatch interpretation
There are 24 start times, nine negative requests (−100to−500kW in 50 kW steps) and three
positive requests (25, 50, 75 kW). For each pair, the assessment cap in intervals is the smaller of
48 and the number of intervals remaining in the local day. The procedure rst tests that cap. If
5
unsuccessful, it searches logarithmically for a feasible duration and checks the following interval.
Standard trials have a 60 s solver limit; selected unresolved boundary trials have existing 300 s
checks.
A trial with an audited feasible incumbent is evidence that its duration can be delivered
under the model. A trial without an incumbent can be either proven infeasible or unresolved;
these outcomes are retained separately. During the search, an unsuccessful trial can narrow the
region explored even when it is unresolved. Consequently the results should be interpreted as
the feasible durations found by this procedure, rather than as an exhaustive exploration of every
duration.
The output agboundary_verifiedcovers both a feasible assessment cap and a proven-
infeasible adjacent candidate. It is not, by itself, a proof that all longer durations are infeasible.
Since extending an event changes the recovery boundary and undisturbed terminal reference,
monotonic feasibility with duration has not been established for this formulation. The main paper
therefore reports certied feasible durations. It retains plus signs for the thirteen unresolved
adjacent cases and explains the cap convention separately. Unmarked cells do not acquire a
stronger global-maximality interpretation merely because the next tested interval was infeasible.
Blank cells mean that this search certied no positive duration.
The accepted dispatch minimises signed event-plus-recovery electricity cost subject to the
request. Where a solve terminates with a nonzero objective gap, it is an accepted incumbent
for that objective, not a proven minimum-cost allocation. Even a proven optimum need not be
unique. Thus component panels illustrate feasible coordinated mechanisms and exact electrical
accounting; they are neither a unique attribution nor an independent valuation of the assets. The
dashed target shown in retained plots applies during the event only, even where the graphical
line extends into post-event space.

S5 Numerical checks and solution quality
The retained manuscript run record identies Python 3.12.0, Pyomo 6.10.1 and HiGHS 1.15.1,
on Windows 11 with an eight-core AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D. Annual scenarios request a relative
MILP gap of 0.1%, with a standard daily time limit of 300 s and a normal acceptance threshold
of 1%. Feasible exceptions in sensitivity cases are disclosed below rather than treating every solve
as proven optimal. The main paper's 5.050% is computed from the retained annual settlements,
not by summing daily objective bounds.
All fourteen annual summaries contain 365 local dates and 35,040 committed intervals. Their
stored checks cover timestamp continuity, physical state hando, workload conservation, storage
mode exclusivity and signed-price settlement reconciliation. The stated audit tolerances are
10−5 in the native state units,10 −3 kW for power ows and10 −7 CPU-hours for work. Central
operation also stores a cohort execution trace. The two central time-limited daily solutions, on
25 May and 4 October, have reported relative gaps of approximately 0.605% and 0.385%, within
the normal threshold.
