Method

Dates are selected at evenly spaced positions across the 2025 standard-day sequence. The 23- and 25-period daylight-saving dates are excluded from the fixed 24-hour model. Every run uses 24 observed IMRP prices and the following settlement day's first three observed prices for the extension horizon.

The wrapper calls only the existing Scenario 1 nominal calculation and Scenario 2 optimisation workflow, including its component cost accounting.
