How much grid flexibility can a data centre deliver — and what does it save?

This dashboard explores a mixed-integer optimisation model of a 1 MW-class data centre. It quantifies the cost of running the site, the savings from shifting flexible compute and dispatching on-site storage, and the demand-response duration the site can sustain across the day.
